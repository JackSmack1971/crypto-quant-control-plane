**Run ID:** ENA-PIT-DQ-20261023-01  
**As-of / retrieval:** 2026-10-23 03:36:47 UTC for price payload archive; Blockscout current state sampled at block timestamp 2026-10-23 03:34:59 UTC.  
**Research scope:** Public data only. ENA spot price, BTC and ETH spot benchmarks, USDe supply, protocol revenue, executed ENA buybacks, and effective circulating float. No account, balance, order, or wallet-portfolio queries were made. No regression was fit.

## Executive status

**Data readiness: PARTIAL.** A full daily vendor-aggregated price panel was returned and archived for ENA, BTC, and ETH. Price identity is resolved, but historical publication and revision clocks are missing, and two vendors disagree materially on aligned daily observations. USDe contract identity is verified; only three historical mainnet supply snapshots and one latest indexed snapshot were obtained, not a continuous or cross-chain series. Public documentation supplies a few revenue and buyback claims, but not PIT daily revenue or independently verified fill records. Effective float remains undefined and unmeasured.

**PIT eligibility:** The downloaded histories are *current historical extracts*, not archived as-observed vintages. Their event/date stamps do not prove when each value first became available or which value an analyst would have seen then. Do not use the vendor extracts as historically PIT features without first-availability and revision evidence.

### Price-source reconciliation

The 90 common daily timestamps were compared by `date` using the absolute percentage difference `100 × |CoinGecko − CoinPaprika| / CoinPaprika`. Both sources returned USD-labelled points at 00:00 UTC, but do not expose comparable construction details (underlying exchanges, sampling/aggregation rule, or as-of cutoff); the differences are therefore a **provider-disagreement diagnostic**, not a finding that either value is the executable market price.

| Asset | Matched days | Absolute gap ≥0.5% | Absolute gap ≥2% | Median absolute gap | 95th percentile | Maximum |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ENA | 90 | 79/90 | 45/90 | 2.01% | 6.65% | 14.32% |
| BTC | 90 | 45/90 | 8/90 | 0.54% | 2.29% | 4.68% |
| ETH | 90 | 44/90 | 12/90 | 0.47% | 2.65% | 4.32% |

The project’s source-quality rule calls for investigation above 0.5% for comparable major-asset quotes and 2% for illiquid-asset quotes. The observed gaps cross those screening levels, but comparability itself is not established because vendor timestamp/price construction details are absent. **Do not average the series.** The next price qualification step is a same-venue spot candle panel (e.g., one named exchange, exact symbol, UTC candle-close convention) plus independent USD conversion or matched quote-unit returns.

### Disposition and highest-value next acquisition

1. **Prices:** preserve this archived daily aggregation as a discovery/coverage series, but acquire same-venue ENAUSDT, BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT UTC spot candles with exchange instrument identifiers and retrieval logs. Reconcile against a second independent venue and keep USDT returns distinct from USD.
2. **USDe supply:** reconstruct finalized Ethereum `Transfer` mint/burn events and check sampled states against historical `totalSupply()`; then separately account for bridge adapters and supported L2 representations. Record block hash, block timestamp, finality policy, retrieval time and reorg/indexer corrections. The current run only samples three historical dates.
3. **Revenue:** obtain the original daily component ledger, metric definitions (fees, gross revenue, Foundation remittance, net revenue, incentives/distributions), calculation version, first-publication timestamps and every revision. The 705-day coverage statement is not a substitute for the rows.
4. **Buybacks:** request/pull transaction IDs plus dates, quantities, venue, execution prices and destination treatment. Where fills occurred on centralized venues through market makers, on-chain treasury transfers alone will not verify execution; keep issuer-reported totals in a separate claim series.
5. **Float:** freeze a definition first, then build point-in-time inclusion/exclusion rules and wallet labels with evidence dates. Report bounds for uncertain attribution and distinguish circulating, unlocked, staked, custody-held, treasury-held and actually tradable supply.

**No predictive model has been run.** Missingness rates for revenue, executed buybacks and effective float are explicitly not measurable from the available evidence, rather than zero.
